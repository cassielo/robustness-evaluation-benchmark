How it works

Upload your model / attack

We accept user uploads of their own model or attack to test. Users can submit their own model or attack anytime and anywhere.

Benchmark evaluation

Each submission is evaluated against our benchmark of 13 attacks and 6 models, matched to the type and dataset selected.

Results by email

When the evaluation is complete, a report of the robustness results is sent to the email address provided.
