    The table shows the attack accuracy of the six models and the right side of it is the deviation of each accuracy of attacks Figure 6 display Table 2 as palette so that we can easily realize the attack rates The color depends on how robust the model is. The more orange, the lower accurate of the attack; the more purple, the higher accurate of the attack. On the three datasets used, CIFAR 10 yielded the highest attack accuracy, whereas the All CNN model exhibited poor defense capability against various attacks. On MNIST, the C&W model was more robust than simple CNN, whereas on ImageNet, InceptionV3 had the highest defensive capability and Resnet 50 had the lowest.

      The bar graph plots the robustness of the six models used in our experiment. It shows that the C&W model delivered the best robustness against the 13 kinds of attacks, followed by InceptionV3. ResNet50 yielded the worst performance with a negative score. As our algorithm is not designed to compare model, but to calculate the relationship between an attack and the model, the greater the numbers of attacks and models we use, the more reliable the results obtained are.
